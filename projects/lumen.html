LUMEN

A third-person dark-fantasy horror game where your lamp, your only tool and your only friend are the same thing: Sol, a living light you can throw. I led it from a playable prototype to seven areas and a plan for Steam and mobile, then put it on hold in November 2026.

Overview

You play the Keeper, in the dark beneath the city of Lumen, with something always following. The fear comes from being chased, not from fighting back. Early on you find Sol, and from then on the light is the whole game: throw Sol into a street light or a receptor to light a bridge or hold up a platform, call it back, and keep moving before the darkness closes in.

The game is seven areas. A is the opening chase, a quiet painted wall and the shrine where you find Sol. It leads into F, a hub of islands joined by light bridges, with a great door across its north end. From F you take on four areas in any order. Each one ends by lighting its lighthouse, whose beam lights a quarter of the great door. Light all four and the door opens onto the city.

What I did

  • Led the design and decided what went in. For the second version I redesigned the game from ten linear chapters into seven areas around a hub, and wrote the areas up as design docs with sketches.
  • Designed the new areas' core ideas: the Lantern Hall's platforms that only hold while their light does, the Crystal Defence's gun that steers crowds by lighting their paths, and a Red Light Green Light room watched by one giant eye.
  • Ran it like a release: a production plan with Steam deadlines, pitch sessions to bring on writing, art and marketing help, and playtest builds with a feedback form.
  • Set it up to ship: separate playtest and release builds, checkpoints before every pressure sequence, and every third-party asset CC0 and licence-checked.

The areas

  • A. The Pursuit, the Painted Wall, Sol: a chase down a street of lamps, a mural that lights up as you walk it, and Sol's cage.
  • F. The Bridges: the hub. Throw Sol to light a bridge, cross, call it back.
  • B. The Lantern Hall: letter-shaped platforms over the dark, each held up by its street light. Fall, and the Behemoth below catches you.
  • C. The Crystal Defence: carry a giant crystal to a dead lighthouse, then defend it from a gun at the top of the cave.
  • D. Red Light Green Light: move while the eye is dark, hide when its light sweeps the trench.
  • E. Bridge of Bridges: crystal-powered pods on wires, and mini Behemoths that scream if they see you.
  • G. The great door: with every quarter lit, it opens onto the city under the eclipse.

How it was built

It started from a game design doc written with a design team, and the second version was made with a small team. The code was written with Claude Code from my design docs. Every third-party asset is CC0, and its source is logged.

Status

On hold since November 2026. Every area is playable from start to finish, and most of the art is still placeholder.
