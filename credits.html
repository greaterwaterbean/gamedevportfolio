Credits & Assets

This site is hand-built HTML/CSS/JS. The pixel-art UI chrome and every sprite, effect, and sound in the homepage's game layer come from the free itch.io packs below. Most are CC0 (public domain) and need no credit; they're all credited anyway. Full thanks to their creators.

Sprites & effects

16x16 DungeonTileset II

The Player 1 knight, the critters on the project cards, the footer patrol, the coins and treasure chest, the lever, and the lava fountains by the logbook.

By 0x72 — 0x72.itch.io/dungeontileset-ii

Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Used with modifications (animation frames combined into sprite strips; the two sword cursors are rotated weapon sprites).

Ninja Adventure — Asset Pack

The fly-by bat, butterflies, the mice on the Tails of the Tower card, emote bubbles, section flags, the campfire, sparkles, click bursts, slash and smoke effects, snowfall, the d20, monster health bars, and all of the (opt-in) sound effects.

By Pixel-Boy and AAA — pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack

Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Used with modifications (frames re-cut into strips; sounds trimmed and converted to MP3).

Pet Cats Pack

The cat napping on the About card.

By LuizMelo — luizmelo.itch.io/pet-cat-pack

Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Used with modifications (cropped into sprite strips).

Crow Sprite

The crow perched on the Devlog logbook, and its "CAW".

By Smithy Games — smithygames.itch.io/crow-sprite

Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Used with modifications (cropped into sprite strips).

Pixel Magic Effects

The portal that opens during the cheat-code party mode.

Coins & Gems & Chests & More

The spinning coins that rain down in party mode.
